Astral Boom

Get help with cosmic gem matching, privateer abilities, controls, and Game Center.

Getting started

How do I play?

Move your ship, collect and place gems, and create matching groups to clear the playfield. Chain reactions and special gems can trigger additional effects.

What are privateers?

Choose from Volt, Blaze, Frost, Flux, Orbit, and Prism. Each privateer brings a different ability to your runs.

How do I use Game Center?

Sign in to Game Center in your device settings if you want to use supported leaderboard features. Availability of features may vary by game version.

Troubleshooting

Restart the game and ensure iOS is up to date. For Game Center problems, confirm that you are signed in and have a network connection. Avoid uninstalling the game before checking whether your progress is backed up.
