Game support

Astral Boom

Get help with cosmic gem matching, Privateer abilities, Game Center leaderboards & achievements, audio/haptics, and controllers.

Getting started

How do I play?

Swipe left or right across the 10 columns to steer your starfighter. Swipe down (or tap Grab) to pull matching gems into your hold from the bottom of a column (up to 6 at once), move to your target lane, and swipe up (or tap Fire) to launch them upward and clear groups of 4 or more connected gems of the same colour. Chain cascades, 2x Power Surges, and Special Gems (Lightning, Fire, Freeze, Laser Sight, and Nuke) boost your score and clear cosmic hazards.

What are Privateers?

Choose from Volt, Blaze, Frost, Flux, Orbit, and Prism. Clearing gems charges your Privateer's weapon meter—when it reaches 100%, double-tap in Portrait gesture mode (or tap the glowing Power button) to unleash that pilot's signature board-clearing ability.

How do Game Center leaderboards and achievements work?

Sign in to Apple Game Center in your iPhone's Settings → Game Center to post high scores to global leaderboards and unlock achievements. You can still play 100% offline anytime—local high scores are saved on-device, and queued scores sync when Game Center is available.

Does Astral Boom support controllers and haptics?

Yes! Astral Boom features custom Apple Core Haptics on compatible iPhones (toggleable anytime under Settings → Audio & Haptics) and supports Bluetooth game controllers including MFi, Xbox, and PlayStation DualSense controllers.

Troubleshooting

  • Game Center scores or achievements not updating: Confirm you are signed in under iOS Settings → Game Center and have an active internet connection, then launch Astral Boom so any queued scores can sync.
  • No sound or haptics: Check your iPhone Silent Mode / Ring setting and device volume, and verify that SFX, Music, and Haptics are enabled in Settings → Audio & Haptics.
  • Performance or display issues: Close and relaunch Astral Boom and ensure your iPhone is running a current version of iOS (iOS 15.0 or later). Avoid uninstalling the app if you want to preserve your local settings and local arcade high-score table.

Contact support

Need help or want to report a bug? Email gamesupport@velocedigital.top with your iPhone model, iOS version, game version (e.g. 1.0.0), and a short description of the issue. Do not include passwords or sensitive Apple Account details.
