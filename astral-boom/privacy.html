Astral Boom Privacy Policy

Effective & Last Updated: November 11, 2026

Summary: Veloce Digital Canada does not collect, store on external servers, track, or sell your personal data. Gameplay settings and local scores stay on your device, and optional leaderboard scores and achievements are handled directly by Apple Game Center.

Overview

Veloce Digital Canada ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes Astral Boom. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play Astral Boom or contact us for support.

On-device game data

Veloce Digital Canada does not operate external game servers or collect personal information, device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics from Astral Boom.

Gameplay preferences (such as audio, music, and haptics settings), local arcade high-score entries (3-letter initials and scores), and any offline-queued scores awaiting Game Center sync are saved locally inside the app's sandboxed storage on your iPhone. None of this data is transmitted to Veloce Digital Canada. If you enable standard Apple iCloud or Finder/iTunes device backups, Apple may include local app storage in your personal device backup.

Apple Game Center

Astral Boom integrates with Apple Game Center (via Apple's GameKit framework) for leaderboards and achievements. When you are signed in to Game Center on your device, the app submits your high scores and achievement progress directly to Apple so they can be displayed in Game Center.

Apple processes your Game Center profile, player identifier, scores, and achievement activity under Apple's Privacy Policy. Veloce Digital Canada does not receive, export, or store your Apple Account details or Game Center identifiers on any developer server. You can manage or sign out of Game Center at any time in your iPhone's Settings → Game Center.

Analytics, advertising, and third-party services

Astral Boom contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics or crash-reporting libraries, and no cross-app tracking. App Store purchases and downloads are processed directly by Apple, and we never receive your payment card or billing details.

Support requests

If you email us for help, we receive the information you choose to send (such as your email address, iPhone model, iOS version, and issue description). We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and troubleshoot issues. We never sell support contact details or use them for marketing. Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to resolve your request (up to 12 months for support reference) or deleted earlier upon request.

Children's privacy

Astral Boom is a general-audience arcade puzzle game and does not collect personal information from any player of any age, including children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Parents and guardians can manage or restrict Apple Game Center features using iOS Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions.

Your choices and data deletion

  • On-device data: Uninstall Astral Boom at any time to remove all locally stored settings and local high-score records from your device.
  • Game Center data: Manage your Game Center profile, privacy visibility, or sign-out state in iOS Settings → Game Center, or manage Apple data via privacy.apple.com.
  • Support emails: Contact us at the address below if you would like any prior support emails deleted.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the game's features change. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
