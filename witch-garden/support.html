Witch Garden

Grow a Little Magic! Find help with planting, harvesting, crafting, and managing your enchanted garden.

Getting started

How do I plant seeds?

Open Plant, select a seed, then choose an available garden plot.

How do I water and harvest?

Use Water to tend growing plants. Once a crop is ready, choose Harvest and select it.

What can I do with my harvest?

Use harvested ingredients for brewing, selling, and fulfilling available contracts. Explore the shop, upgrades, decorations, and visiting merchant for more options.

Troubleshooting

If the game becomes unresponsive, close and reopen it. Check for available updates. Before deleting the app, be aware that locally stored progress may be lost.

Contact support

For bugs or questions, email SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE. Include your device model, iOS version, game version, and steps to reproduce the problem. Please do not include passwords or sensitive personal information.
